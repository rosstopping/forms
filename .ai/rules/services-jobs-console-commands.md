# Services Jobs Console Commands

## Opt-in scheduled competitor research supersedes on-demand-only audits
Growth and Complete may explicitly enable research or drafts on the content plan; existing plans default to manual. Research runs through competitors:dispatch-research, bounded by owner subscription cadence/sample limits and a locked plan reservation; queued stages recheck eligibility. Reads and content generation never launch paid research. Drafts select saved relevant briefs through the existing content schedule and weekly allowance, preserving cooldowns, review and SEO impact tracking; research-only never automatically prepares drafts. Compare fetched own pages and matching stored competitor evidence, retain source provenance, and keep ranking explanations hypothetical.
