## Keep search landing pages distinct and connected
Commercial SEO landing pages use root-level, intent-specific URLs and unique copy/metadata while feature pages remain product explanations. Every landing page must have a canonical URL, FAQ schema, sitemap entry, service-hub inbound link, and contextual links to adjacent landing intents.
