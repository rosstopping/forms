## Keep shared growth plans short static and visually led
Shared plans are standalone protected documents in the search audit's warm white/coral/ink visual language, with no workspace chrome, links, forms, disclosures, scripts or other interactive controls. Keep the intro to one short sentence, three action/outcome priorities, at most six focus searches, and six brief monthly milestones. Enforce concise AI fields; compact legacy report copy at presentation without changing stored evidence. Include print/A4 styling and keep generation/sharing controls in the admin workspace only.
