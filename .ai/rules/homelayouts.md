# Homelayouts

## Keep the homepage concise and pricing on the pricing page
The homepage should use concise, concrete service copy. Do not show plan prices, pricing sections, upfront-cost promotions or the client list headed 'Some of the businesses we look after'. Keep navigation links to Pricing; show the shared promotional pricing banner only on the pricing route.

## Keep the selected Spotlight visual system
Use the chosen Spotlight direction across the homepage: white canvas, warm #faf7f4 panels, near-black #151618 headings, #62666d body copy and coral #d63d24 actions (#ff5035 badge ring). Use the existing Inter treatment, medium-weight tight headings, rounded-3xl panels and pill controls; avoid the earlier green/serif/dark-section aesthetic. Keep the homepage near the nav, with measured breathing room rather than tall empty sections. Align content to max-w-7xl and use responsive, readable layouts.

## Preserve the chosen hero message and audit form
Keep the compact outlined badge with coral ring and “We’ll get you more customers.”; main headline “Turn more searches into your next customer.” with restrained coral underline. Description explains Google, ChatGPT and sites shaping AI answers. The website field and coral “Get your free search audit” submit button form one rounded control (short “Free audit” label on mobile); submit directly to the existing protected anonymous audit flow. Keep inline errors, CSRF, honeypot, configured Turnstile and attribution. Keep “I don’t have a website yet” linking to Contact; no no-email/no-commitment reassurance below the form.

## Keep homepage content concise and credible
Avoid repeated sales pitches, repeated audit forms, invented results, ranking badges or customer logos. Retain the real Loom walkthrough, Ross/Doncaster/phone, weekly updates and website ownership information. Search/AI marks are locally hosted monochrome Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude, evenly arranged 2/3/6 columns; label them as places people look for answers, not partnerships or all-provider monitoring. Preserve SEO service/journal links, contact/booking access and pricing navigation, with no prices or VAT qualifiers on the homepage. Comparison UI and unused variants are removed after selection.

## Use concrete service copy and a restrained video player
Avoid numbered 1–2–3 process cards on the homepage: use a simple split layout describing actual page copy, content and technical work. Prefer unbranded video playback when an owned video file is supplied. Until then retain the real walkthrough with supported Loom title/owner/share/top-bar hiding parameters; do not claim these remove all Loom branding or substitute a fabricated product demo.

## Use a single homepage conversion action
Homepage CTAs must use “Get your free search audit”. The hero submits its website URL directly; CTA links without an input go to marketing.free-site-audit (/get-started), which asks for the URL. Remove booking, feature-exploration and no-website alternatives from homepage conversion sections. This supersedes earlier instructions to keep the no-website link or homepage booking access. Keep ordinary navigation/footer links and founder contact information; other marketing pages are outside this restriction.

## Show the homepage walkthrough full width without promotional copy
The homepage walkthrough spans the max-w-7xl content width in one column with responsive page gutters and rounded corners. Remove the adjoining heading and paragraph, including “Your progress, in plain sight” and “A clear weekly update…”. Do not add another sales caption or explanatory panel around the video. This supersedes earlier split-panel and weekly-update copy guidance for this section.
