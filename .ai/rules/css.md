# Css

## Use the shared Spotlight style on public marketing pages
All public marketing layouts use marketing-site and prospect-workspace. Scope the coral/warm-white colour and Inter font tokens to this surface; do not change admin/account themes. Use compact rounded warm hero panels, medium sans headings, pill actions and restrained spacing. Main navigation is Services, How it works, Pricing and About plus the search-audit CTA; use a mobile disclosure, and keep agencies/guides/technical pages in the footer. Preserve focused PPC navigation, form flows, pricing offers, legal content and agency concept labels. Prefer concrete copy and remove repeated feature explanations and generic closing pitches.
