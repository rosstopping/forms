# Views

## Keep SEO data supplier names out of customer copy
Do not mention DataForSEO by name in public marketing pages, customer website workspaces, or customer emails. Use plain labels such as Google search estimates, desktop ranking checks, and backlink estimates. Continue distinguishing third-party estimates from verified Search Console data; internal service names, config keys, and admin diagnostics may remain technical.
