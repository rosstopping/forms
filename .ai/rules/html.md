## Customer review invitations use website branding
Review invitations are customer-facing business emails: use the invitation's snapshotted from_name as the HTML title/header and plain-text heading, with no Sitewell logo, tagline, footer or marketing links. This is an exception to shared Sitewell email branding; other system emails retain the default brand.
