## Personal audit reviews replace the copy-only email offer
Completed anonymous reports offer Ross's three personal priorities within one working day, inline below the summary and through a labelled primary fixed action; booking a call is secondary. This supersedes the copy-only/icon email and booking-primary rules. Explicit personal review requests stay on WebsiteAudit, create no account, queue a report acknowledgement and owner deadline notice, and are fulfilled by an admin sending exactly three priorities once. Track replies, actual calls and paying clients manually; queued email is not proof of delivery. Ongoing marketing consent is optional, versioned and separate; signed confirmation withdrawals clear consent across audits for the same email. Do not enrol public audit leads into the existing SaaS lifecycle or cold outreach sequences.
